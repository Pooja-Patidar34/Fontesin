# Weekly Progress Report

**Project:** Fontesin Platform
**Week:** May 25 – May 30, 2026

---

Dear Client,

Here is a summary of the progress we made on the Fontesin platform this week. This week's focus was on strengthening account security, adding a new way for members to grow within the platform, and improving the overall look and ease of use across the site.

## What we delivered this week

### 1. New "Become a Coordinator" upgrade process
Business users can now request to be upgraded to a Coordinator directly from their profile. When they apply, your administrators receive the request and can review and either approve or decline it, with the option to add a reason. The member is automatically notified of the decision. This creates a clear, self-service path for members to take on a bigger role on the platform.

### 2. Stronger identity verification at sign-up
We tightened the registration process so that new users must complete identity verification before they can choose a plan or access the dashboard. This closes a gap where someone could previously skip the verification step. Existing members are unaffected — they continue as normal without needing to re-verify.

### 3. Plan selection improvements
New members are now properly guided to select a plan after verifying their identity, and we corrected the records linking business users to their companies so everything is consistent.

### 4. Region pages
We added and refreshed the country pages (United States, Germany, Czech Republic, UAE, Japan), updated the interactive world map, and introduced a "Coming Soon" page for regions not yet live.

### 5. Visual and usability improvements
We made significant improvements to the look and usability of several key areas, including:
- The **Users** management screen
- The **Companies** and **Plans** pages
- The **Commissions** section
- Site navigation and the **Invoice** layout

### 6. Bug fixes and refinements
We resolved a number of smaller issues reported during the week, including pop-up behavior and certification-related fixes, to make the experience smoother.

## In short
This week the platform became more secure, easier to navigate, and added a new growth path for members through the Coordinator upgrade feature.

- Continued polish based on any feedback you share.
