Legal Business Forms

The following legal structures are available for business registration in Japan. Each form has distinct liability, taxation, and governance characteristics.

1
KK (Kabushiki Kaisha)
Joint-stock company; most prestigious form; min. ¥1 capital.
2
GK (Godo Kaisha)
LLC equivalent; simpler structure; lower setup costs.
3
Gomei Kaisha
General partnership; all partners have unlimited liability.
4
Goshi Kaisha
Limited partnership; mix of general and limited partners.
5
Branch Office
Foreign company extension; no separate legal entity.
