Legal Business Forms

The following legal structures are available for business registration in United Arab Emirates. Each form has distinct liability, taxation, and governance characteristics.

1
LLC (Limited Liability Company)
Most common form; requires local sponsor or 100% foreign ownership in free zones.
2
Free Zone Company (FZC/FZE)
100% foreign ownership; tax exemptions; limited to free zone operations.
3
Branch of Foreign Company
Extension of parent company; requires local service agent.
4
Civil Company
For professional services (law, medicine, engineering).
5
Sole Establishment
Single owner; unlimited liability; for small businesses.
