Legal Business Forms

The following legal structures are available for business registration in United States. Each form has distinct liability, taxation, and governance characteristics.

1
LLC (Limited Liability Company)
Most popular form for SMEs; flexible taxation and limited liability.
2
C-Corporation
Standard corporate structure; suitable for venture-backed companies.
3
S-Corporation
Pass-through taxation; max 100 shareholders.
4
Sole Proprietorship
Simplest form; no legal separation from owner.
5
Partnership (LP/LLP)
Two or more partners; limited or general liability options.
