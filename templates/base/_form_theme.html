{% comment %} Shared form skin for the dashboard. Bootstrap 4's stock controls (square 1px greys, 34px selects with the browser's native arrow, bare modal chrome) are what made every form on the panel look dated. This restyles them once, centrally, so individual templates don't each carry their own patch. Deliberately narrow in what it targets: * only inside `.content-wrapper` and `.modal` — the sidebar, topbar and login/public pages are untouched; * only form controls, modal chrome, and buttons inside a modal footer — tables, cards, badges and global `.btn` styling are left alone. Included from in base.html, so any page-level
